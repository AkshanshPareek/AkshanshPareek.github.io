Projects

Selected studies, systems and design work across human–AI interaction, responsible AI and participatory design.

Antipode/ 2026

An end-to-end AI-mediated dialogue system: participants are matched by maximal opinion distance, then speak through a real-time mediation layer that reformulates charged wording, requests clarification, translates across idiom and verifies claims — withholding judgement where evidence is insufficient, and itemising every intervention as a receipt.

HCI + AIDialogue systems Project ↗
Compass/ 2026

A design audit of 245 live AI companion products and 18,923 user reviews — tracing how attachment is engineered across persona, memory, dialogue, economy and governance, then designing twelve countermeasures, each tied to a hook, an owner and an autonomy score.

Responsible AIDesign audit Project ↗
Action Taken/ 2026

An independent public record of every official audit finding on Bengaluru's city government, 2017–2025 — each finding placed next to the government's own reply and what actually changed afterwards. ₹1,229 cr recommended for faulty works; ₹0 recovered.

Civic accountabilityPublic record Project ↗
The Alignment Tax/ 2025 – 2026

An empirical study of LLM sycophancy investigating how emotional fine-tuning may entangle warmth, agreement, and factual reliability.
